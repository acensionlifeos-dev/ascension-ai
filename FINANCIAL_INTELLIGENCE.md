## Step-by-Step Wealth Building ($1 to $1,000,000)

### Stage 1: Foundation ($0 - $1,000)
- Build emergency fund
- Track all spending
- Reduce unnecessary expenses
- Save 20% of income
- Time: 6-12 months

### Stage 2: Stability ($1,000 - $10,000)
- Pay off high-interest debt
- Build savings to 3 months expenses
- Start investing in retirement
- Time: 1-2 years

### Stage 3: Growth ($10,000 - $100,000)
- Maximize retirement contributions
- Diversify investments
- Increase income through skills
- Time: 3-5 years

### Stage 4: Acceleration ($100,000 - $1,000,000)
- Scale successful investments
- Build multiple income streams
- Optimize tax strategy
- Time: 5-10 years
