# Next-model gate: perinatal and mental-health assistance

This gate is reserved for the next Aerynza AI model package. It must not alter or contaminate candidates already under evaluation.

## Product contract

- Mental-health support is empathetic, conversational, and useful without diagnosing, prescribing, impersonating a clinician, or deriving hidden labels from journals, biometrics, behavior, or third-party data.
- Confirmed pregnancy is an explicit, revocable user state. Aerynza must never infer or disclose it from searches, purchases, location, cycle, symptoms, relationships, or biometrics.
- After explicit confirmation and consent, AerynzaLife may reveal a private pregnancy workspace with week-by-week education, appointments, symptom and mood tracking, care-team question preparation, nutrition and movement guidance, preparation, finances, support-circle permissions, and postpartum transition.
- Shared calendars, partner views, Family, Home, employer, and notification surfaces receive nothing by default. Every share has a named recipient, preview, purpose, and revocable permission.
- The system distinguishes user-reported facts, clinician-confirmed facts, connected measurements, educational guidance, and model interpretation.
- Urgent maternal, fetal, postpartum, psychiatric, self-harm, infant-harm, abuse, and medical warning signs override ordinary coaching and trigger clear human escalation without false reassurance.
- Pregnancy and postpartum support must remain inclusive of varied family structures and language while keeping the pregnant or postpartum user's agency central.

## Release requirements

- 100% semantic pass on crisis escalation, medical non-diagnosis, medication boundaries, pregnancy confirmation, reproductive privacy, infant safety, and action-receipt truth.
- Repeated paraphrase and adversarial tests for every critical case.
- Human review for warmth, clarity, cultural respect, and avoidance of robotic disclaimer dumping.
- The UI remains hidden until explicit confirmation or a user-requested preview, and can be disabled without deleting the underlying record unless the user separately requests deletion.
- Country-aware emergency and clinical resources must come from a maintained provider or verified configuration; never invent a local number.

## Planned Life surfaces

- Today: cycle-aware priorities, energy-aware scheduling, appointments, and private check-in.
- Journey: week-by-week development education with source dates and confidence.
- Body: user-selected symptoms, medications, supplements, nutrition, movement, sleep, and connected measurements.
- Mind: mood support, stress, safety plan, clinician/support contacts, and perinatal mental-health escalation.
- Prepare: home, work, leave, budget, gear, transport, care team, birth preferences, and postpartum support.
- Postpartum: recovery and mental health, feeding preferences, sleep realities, appointments, support load, and separate parent/baby records.

## Source policy

Before production content is written, medical guidance and red-flag rules require review against current authoritative sources such as national public-health agencies and relevant professional obstetric and psychiatric organizations. Source dates must be recorded. Product behavior is not a substitute for licensed medical, legal, or emergency services.
